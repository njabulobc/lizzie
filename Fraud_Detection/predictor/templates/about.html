Our mission

Elizabank delivers secure digital-first banking experiences across Zimbabwe and the broader SADC region. Our fraud intelligence platform empowers analysts with real-time insights so customers can transact confidently. We combine world-class data science with a customer-first philosophy to stop fraud before it impacts our communities.

History

Founded in 2024, Elizabank has grown from a boutique lender into a regional digital bank delivering inclusive services to individuals and businesses.

Innovation

We invest heavily in AI, behavioural analytics, and automation to ensure suspicious patterns are surfaced instantly for human review.

Community

From financial literacy programs to SME workshops, our outreach is centred on helping customers bank safely and responsibly.

Leadership principles

  • Maintain radical transparency with regulators, partners, and customers.
  • Design experiences that are accessible, intuitive, and empathetic.
  • Champion diversity within our teams to produce resilient outcomes.

What we offer

  • Personal and business banking tailored for digital lifestyles.
  • Lending solutions that respond to customer data in near-real time.
  • Secure mobile and web banking experiences with proactive fraud monitoring.
